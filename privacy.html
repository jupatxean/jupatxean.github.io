JupaFit — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

The short version

JupaFit is a fitness app. To work, it needs to know some things about your body and what you eat, so it asks you and it remembers the answers.

  • We do not sell your data. Not to anyone, for any price, ever.
  • There are no ads in JupaFit, so there is no advertising identifier and nothing to target.
  • Your conversations with Jupa never leave your phone. They are stored on the handset only, never on our servers.
  • Meal photographs are not kept. A photo is sent to be analysed and discarded in the same request; it is never written to storage or a log.
  • Health data never leaves your phone. JupaFit reads your step count and sleep, uses them on the device, uploads neither, and never writes anything back to Apple Health or Health Connect.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app, in Profile → Privacy & data, and it deletes on the server too.

The app also shows this in plain language on the Privacy & data screen, which lists what is on your phone, what is on your account, and what is never collected at all.

2. What we collect, and why

Nothing here is collected for its own sake. Each item below exists because a feature does not work without it.

2.1 What you tell us

When you first open JupaFit it asks you a set of questions. The answers are:

Data Why
Name (or nickname) So the app can address you
Gender, age Calorie and protein maths
Height, weight, preferred units The same maths, and your progress
Goal, activity level, training experience Building your plan
Days per week, training location, equipment Building your plan
Typical daily steps Your starting activity estimate
Free-text notes (optional) Anything you want the plan to account for
Chosen avatar Shown in the app

You can skip the free-text question. Everything else is required for the app to produce a plan at all.

2.2 What you record as you use it

Data Where it is stored
Weigh-ins, body measurements Phone and account
Food logged, and foods you favourite Phone and account
Workout sessions Phone and account
HP, level, streak, missions completed Phone and account
Sleep you typed in yourself Phone and account
Sleep read from Health Phone only — see 2.3
Your last ten food searches Phone only
Your conversation with Jupa Phone only
Today's in-progress state Phone only

"Account" means our database, so that a new phone is not a fresh start. Signing out removes every line of the phone copy from that handset, and so does deleting the app.

2.3 Health and fitness data

With your permission, JupaFit reads two things from Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android), and only two:

  • Step count
  • Sleep analysis

This permission is asked for the first time a feature needs it, and the app works without it — you can decline and keep using JupaFit.

Neither one leaves your phone. This is the part worth reading twice:

  • Steps stay on the handset. They run the daily step mission and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Sleep read from Health stays on the handset too. If you *type in* how long you slept, that number is backed up to your account so your streak survives a new phone — but a night that came from Apple Health or Health Connect is dropped before the upload, by the code that does the uploading rather than by whoever remembers to ask for it.
  • Nothing is ever written back. JupaFit requests read access only; on iOS it does not declare write access at all.
  • Health data is never sent to the AI features, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with anyone at all — it is not in our database, so there is nothing to share.

2.4 Meal photographs

If you photograph a meal, the picture is sent to our server and on to our AI provider to estimate the food and its nutrition. The image is not stored. It exists for the length of one request and is never written to storage or to any log. What is kept is the *result* — the food name and its nutrition numbers — as an entry in your food log.

You can use the whole food log without ever taking a photograph, by searching or entering food by hand.

2.5 Your conversation with the coach

The coach ("Jupa") is an AI feature. Two things happen when you send a message, and they are different from each other:

  • Storage. The conversation is saved on your phone only, under a key scoped to your account so that two people using one handset cannot read each other's. It is never uploaded, never backed up to our database, and it goes when you sign out or delete the app.
  • Processing. To answer, the message and the recent conversation are sent to our AI provider, together with a short summary of your training context so the answer is about you rather than generic. That summary can contain: your weight, your height, your goal, your daily calorie and protein targets, your recent workouts, and the free-text answers you gave about your build and what you find difficult.

It does not contain your name, your email address, your date of birth, or any health data read from Apple Health or Health Connect.

If you would rather not send any of that, do not use the coach; the rest of the app is unaffected.

2.6 Account and sign-in

You sign in with Apple, with Google, or with an email address and password. We receive an account identifier and an email address from whichever you choose. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we receive Apple's relay address and never see your real one.

We never see and never store your password for Apple or Google.

2.7 Subscriptions

If you subscribe, the payment is taken by Apple or Google, not by us. We never see your card number, and we could not charge it if we wanted to. We receive from the store only whether a subscription is active, which plan, and when the period ends.

2.8 Crash reports

When the app crashes we receive a technical report — what went wrong, where in the code, the device model, and the OS version. It is deliberately configured to carry as little as possible: personal information is switched off, no screenshot is captured, no view hierarchy is captured, and log lines are stripped before sending. The report is linked to an anonymous account identifier so that repeated crashes for one person can be recognised as one problem.

3. Who else sees it

We use a small number of processors. Each is here because a feature needs it, and each is bound to use the data only to provide that service.

Processor What reaches them Why
Supabase Your account, and everything in the "account" column above Database, sign-in and backup
OpenAI Meal photographs at the moment of analysis; coach messages and the training summary in 2.5 The AI features
Apple / Google Sign-in identity; subscription status Sign-in and payment
RevenueCat Subscription status and an anonymous account identifier Managing subscriptions across both stores
Sentry Crash reports as described in 2.8 Fixing crashes

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. There is no advertising network, no analytics broker, and no data broker of any kind in this app.

International transfers

These providers operate servers outside the country you are in, so using JupaFit involves transferring your data internationally. Where the law requires a safeguard for that transfer — for example the EU's standard contractual clauses — we rely on the terms each provider offers for it.

4. What we never collect

This list is a promise, not an inventory:

  • Your location. Not once, for any reason. The app requests no location permission of any kind.
  • Advertising identifiers. There are no ads.
  • Your contacts, your calendar, your microphone.
  • Photographs other than the meal you choose to send. The app can read the one picture you pick; it cannot see your photo library.
  • Screenshots of your screens, including when the app crashes.

5. How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you sign out or delete the app, both of which clear it.
  • On your account: until you delete your account.
  • Meal photographs: not kept at all.
  • Crash reports: retained by Sentry on its default schedule and then deleted.

We do not keep an archive of deleted accounts.

6. Deleting everything

Open Profile → Privacy & data → Delete account.

This deletes your row and everything that references it — your questionnaire, your weigh-ins and measurements, your food log and favourites, your workouts, your progression, your sleep record, and your subscription record — and then clears the copy on the handset. It is immediate and it cannot be undone.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through Apple or Google. Cancel that in your App Store or Play Store account settings, or it will keep renewing.

You can also just sign out, which clears the phone and leaves the account intact.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Most of this is already in the app: the questionnaire is editable in the profile, and deletion is one screen away. For anything else, write to the address in section 1.

Where we need a legal basis under the GDPR: we process the data in section 2 to perform the contract of providing the app to you; we process health data (section 2.3) on your explicit consent, given through the operating system's permission prompt and withdrawable at any time in your phone's settings; and we process crash reports on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working.

8. Children

JupaFit is not for children. You must be 13 or older to create an account, and the app will not accept an age below 13. If you believe a child has given us data, write to the address in section 1 and we will delete it.

9. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top, and for anything significant we will say so in the app rather than quietly editing the page.
